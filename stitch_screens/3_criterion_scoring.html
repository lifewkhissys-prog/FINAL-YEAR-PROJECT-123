Literature Review

Evaluating the depth, relevance, and critical synthesis of existing research.

KNUST Rubric Criteria: Literature Review

  • Demonstrates comprehensive understanding of relevant literature.
  • Critically analyzes sources rather than merely summarizing them.
  • Identifies a clear gap in existing research that the thesis addresses.
  • Proper citation and formatting according to institutional guidelines.
